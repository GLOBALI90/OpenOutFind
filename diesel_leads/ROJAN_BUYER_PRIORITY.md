## Quality rules for the collector

- Store the exact public source URL and the evidence snippet/terms; keep “company candidate”, “published RFQ”, “qualified buyer” and “deal” separate.
- Refresh existing company rows by website domain; do not duplicate companies.
- Store public business emails/phone numbers only. Do not guess individual email addresses.
- Keep outreach disabled during this collection batch. Research and human verification first.
- Never represent huge-volume or unverifiable RFQs as confirmed demand; validate buyer identity, product specs, capacity, destination, payment and applicable sanctions/export controls.
